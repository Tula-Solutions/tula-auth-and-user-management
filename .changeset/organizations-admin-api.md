Organizations, first step (ADR 0065): the `organizations` environment settings (off by default), the schemas, role keys and permission grammar of organizations and memberships, six new activity types, and five `organization.*` error codes. `@tula-tech/admin` gains the typed admin routes for organizations and their memberships. `@tula-tech/config` leaves the section out of an environment's fingerprint while it is the default. `@tula-tech/core` carries the new error codes in its generated types.

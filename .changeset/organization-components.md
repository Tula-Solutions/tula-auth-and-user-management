The organization components (Orgs 07). `@tula-tech/react` and `@tula-tech/nextjs` get `<OrganizationSwitcher>` (the active organization, the user's others, the personal account, suggestions from their email domain, an invitation they opened, and the way to create or manage an organization), `<CreateOrganization>` and `<OrganizationProfile>` (General: rename, leave, delete; Members: roles, removal; Invitations: invite, revoke; Domains: claim, verify with the emailed code, what a verified domain does), each drawn only where the user's role permits, themed, localized (the `organizationSwitcher`, `createOrganization` and `organizationProfile` groups) and accessible. They read the new `organizations` member of the public configuration (whether organizations are on, whether users may create them, whether there is a personal account); a configuration without it means organizations are off. `<TulaProvider>` takes `organizationProfileUrl`, `createOrganizationUrl` and `invitationUrl`, and both packages re-export `readInvitationLink`. `@tula-tech/core` gets the calls the screens make: `organizations.update`, `.remove`, `.leave`, `.roles`, `.members.list`, `.members.changeRole`, `.members.remove` and `.domains.list`, `.start`, `.verify`, `.update`, `.remove`. The API gets the client routes behind them under `/v1/client/me/organizations` (rename, delete, the member list, a member's role and removal, the assignable roles); a change of role or a removal that would leave an organization without a member who can manage members is refused with `organization.last_manager`. `@tula-tech/contract` gets `OrganizationMember`, `AssignableRole`, the `organizations` member of `ClientConfig`, and the Zod-free `@tula-tech/contract/organization-permissions` gains `ORGANIZATION_MANAGE_PERMISSION`, `ORGANIZATION_DELETE_PERMISSION` and `MEMBERSHIPS_READ_PERMISSION`, the keys the screens check.
